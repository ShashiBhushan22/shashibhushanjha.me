About Me

Seeking research and doctoral opportunities in wireless communications and emerging 6G systems

I am a Nepalese wireless communications researcher and an M.Tech graduate in Electrical Engineering from IIT Ropar, specializing in Communication and Signal Processing. My work is centered on wireless multiple-access systems, PHY-layer receiver design, and analytical and simulation-based performance evaluation.

My master's thesis, “Generalization of Downlink and Uplink NOMA Framework for Future Generation Technology,” developed a unified BER and throughput framework for multi-user power-domain NOMA over Rayleigh fading. The work examined power allocation, distance-based user ordering, successive interference cancellation, error propagation, and oversampling across downlink and uplink systems.

My broader research interests include 6G and beyond, non-terrestrial networks (NTN), integrated sensing and communications (ISAC), multiple access, and PHY-layer signal processing. I use MATLAB, Python, C++, and Linux for modeling, simulation, and data analysis.
